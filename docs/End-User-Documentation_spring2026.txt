1. About Learn and Help
Learn and Help is a nonprofit program (EIN: 39-5158217) where middle and high school students learn computational thinking and computer programming. Proceeds from this program are used to support schools in need by providing books, educational materials, and resources.
Our Impact So Far:

200 schools supported
3,455 books shipped
151 students empowered

Donations to Learn and Help are tax-deductible as permitted by law. To learn more, visit www.learnandhelp.com.

2. Navigating the Website
The navigation bar at the top of every page gives you quick access to all sections:

About Us: Learn about our mission, team, and nonprofit status.
Learn: Browse available classes and course information.
Blog: Read announcements, news, and updates from our team.
Enroll Now: Sign up for a class.
FAQs: Find answers to frequently asked questions.
Events: View upcoming events and activities.
Help: See our impact, nominate schools, recommend nonprofits, and use our chatbot.
Login / My Account: Log in or manage your account.


3. Creating an Account & Logging In
3.1 Creating an Account

Click Login in the top-right corner of the navigation bar.
On the Login page, look below the password field for the option to create a new account.
Fill in your details and submit the form.
You now have a regular user account.

3.2 Logging In

Click Login in the navigation bar.
Enter your email and password.
Click the Login button.
Once logged in, the navigation bar will update to show My Account and Log Off.


4. Browsing Classes
Click Learn in the navigation bar to explore available courses.
Python 101

Introduction to Python — perfect for absolute beginners with no prior programming experience.
Students can prepare for the AP CS Principles exam and earn PCEP (Certified Entry-Level Python Programmer) certification.
Next batch runs September 2026 to May 2027 on weekends.

Python DS (Data Science)

An advanced course for students who have completed Python 101.
Covers Object-Oriented Programming (OOP), NumPy, Pandas, and visualization tools such as Matplotlib.
Students complete a capstone solo project.
Prerequisite: Python 101.

You can also view instructor profiles on the Learn page for more information about who teaches each course.

5. Enrolling in a Class

Click Enroll Now in the navigation bar.
Review the available classes and select the one you want to join.
Complete the enrollment form with your details.
Submit your enrollment request.


6. Help & Community Impact
Click Help in the navigation bar to access the following:

See Our Impact: View statistics on schools supported, books shipped, and students empowered.
Schools We Supported: Browse the list of schools that have received donations.
Books We Shipped: See the books and educational materials that have been delivered.
Nominate a School: Know a school in need? Submit a nomination for support.
Recommend a Non-Profit: Suggest a nonprofit organization for us to consider partnering with.
Chatbot: Use our built-in chatbot to get quick answers about the program. You can ask questions such as:
  - How many schools does Learn and Help support?
  - Which schools are in Andhra Pradesh?
  - Which school has the most students?
  - Who are the board members?
  - What classes are offered?
  - Tell me about upcoming events.
  The chatbot answers using live data from our database, so responses are always current and accurate.

8. Books & Library
Click Help in the navigation bar, then select Books We Shipped to browse our educational book library.

Over 620 books are available, organized by grade level (Primary School, Upper Primary, High School, and more).
Each entry shows the title, author, publisher, year, page count, and availability status.
These books are distributed to supported schools in India as part of the Learn and Help charitable mission.
